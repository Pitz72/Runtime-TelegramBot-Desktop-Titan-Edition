## 🔥 The "Killer" Features

**🎬 YouTube Zero-Config (No API Key needed)**
Forget complex Google Cloud setups, quotas, or credit cards. Titan uses a native InnerTube integration: just paste the YouTube handle (e.g. `@YourChannel`) and the bot does the rest. It even features a smart **Anti-Premiere Filter** — it ignores scheduled videos and only publishes them when they actually go live.

**🛡️ IronShield Anti-Spam**
Never spam your audience with old news again. The advanced **dual-hash check (URL + Title)** ensures that even if a website changes a link, the bot remembers the post and won't republish it.

**🌙 Quiet Hours & Digest Mode**
Respect your community's sleep. Set **Quiet Hours** to pause publishing during the night — the bot queues everything and neatly publishes it in the morning. Too much news? Use **Digest Mode** to accumulate articles and send them as a single, clean daily summary.
